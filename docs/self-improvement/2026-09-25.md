## MetaHarness loop and measurements

The [frozen plan](2026-09-25-plan.json) and external runner hash fixed the hypothesis, six held-out groups and thresholds before candidate implementation. Baseline scored 1/6. The candidate scored 6/6: exact provenance and source heads, bounded learning-report evidence, router abstention evidence, durable-save and graph-metadata record boundaries, quantize/filter limitations, and preservation of prior records plus the fixed authority surface. Groups 4 and 5 inspect raw snapshot records rather than independent end-to-end journeys; exact search is exercised by groups 2 and 3 and the regression suite. One candidate wording was tightened after the unchanged evaluator rejected the searchable phrase “RuVnet learning” even in a negated sentence; no evaluator or threshold changed.

All 17 candidate test groups and both marketplace validators passed on Node 22 and 24. The first Node 22 invocation inherited proxy variables and emitted an experimental runtime warning on stderr, causing the negative CLI assertion to fail; the same exact suite passed after removing those environmental variables. Four skills, six MCP tools and three resources remained fixed. MetaHarness 0.4.16 retained 6/6 hard constraints; its heuristic scores are not proof of improvement. The package contained 18 files, compressed to 31,437 bytes, and its normalized 94-entry dependency closure was byte-identical to baseline.

Fifteen deterministic alternating pairs used 10,000 exact-search calls and 20 cold CLI starts per arm per pair. Baseline hot p95 was 0.133902 ms and candidate hot p95 was 0.160842 ms: +20.119%, +0.026940 ms. The frozen rule required both at most +20% relative and +0.05 ms absolute, so the 0.119 percentage-point relative miss rejects the candidate. Cold p95 rose 0.0919% (+0.0609 ms); RSS rose 131,072 bytes. Both passed. [Raw paired measurements](2026-09-25-benchmarks.json) retain every sample. The threshold was not reinterpreted and no outcome-driven rerun was made.

Pinned Ruflo 3.25.6 deep scanning and npm audit reported zero known high or critical findings. Scanner coverage is limited, so this is not a complete-security claim. An independent review confirmed the rejection: passing the absolute, cold and RSS gates cannot override the conjunctive hot-p95 failure. Since the local gate failed, no candidate branch was pushed and no candidate CI was requested.

## Forecast

Prediction, 81% confidence by 2027-01-23: agent-memory and retrieval promotion suites will require state-conditioned, set-level evidence sufficiency, so individually relevant but collectively incomplete top-k results cannot pass. It passes only if at least two primary benchmarks or reviewed production harnesses first published or materially revised after 2026-09-25 include grouped evidence requirements, a held-out split, an exact completeness or coverage metric, and a downstream missing-group ablation. Fewer than two falsifies it. No prior forecast horizon expired today.

Established results are narrower. [SERBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20050) scores whether a retrieved set covers every fact required by a captured agent state and includes a missing-group downstream ablation; its [official implementation](https://github.com/LordTARN1SHED/SERBench) separates public calibration from held-out evaluation. [MESA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10108) reports task-adaptive composition of complementary memory structures. [IFCMemoryBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26072) and the official [STATE-Bench agent-learning track](https://github.com/microsoft/STATE-Bench/blob/main/docs/AGENT_LEARNING_TRACK.md) further motivate evaluation beyond isolated top-k relevance. These sources motivate the prediction; they do not prove future adoption or justify lowering the 100-record gate.
